I am a full-time Ph.D. Research Scholar at the Department of English, Rashtrasant Tukadoji Maharaj Nagpur University, Nagpur, Maharashtra, India. My academic interests lie in American literature, African American literature, postcolonial studies, race and identity, and contemporary literary criticism.

My doctoral research examines the evolution of race and social commentary in the novels of Colson Whitehead, with particular attention to the representation of power, institutionalised racism, historical memory, and social inequality.

Alongside my research, I am engaged in teaching English literature at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels. I also have an interest in open-source software development, exploring technology, programming, and digital tools through independent projects.

## Research interests

- American literature

- Postcolonial studies
- Race and identity
- Contemporary literary criticism
- Digital tools and the humanities

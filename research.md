## Doctoral research

My Ph.D. research examines the evolution of race and social commentary in the novels of **Colson Whitehead**. It pays particular attention to four themes:

- **Power**: how authority and its structures are represented in the fiction
- **Institutionalised racism**: the systems, not only the individuals, that sustain racial hierarchy
- **Historical memory**: how the past is recovered, reimagined and contested
- **Social inequality**: the material and social conditions that shape the characters' lives
